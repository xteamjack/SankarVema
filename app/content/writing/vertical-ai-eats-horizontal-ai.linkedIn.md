The broad, do-everything AI assistant is becoming a commodity. The durable value is in AI that goes deep into one industry.

The first wave of AI products was horizontal: one assistant, for everyone, for everything. Impressive, and increasingly a commodity, because your competitor has the same one. Value is moving the other way, into vertical AI: systems deep in a single industry, carrying the data, workflow and judgment only that domain has.

WHY DEPTH WINS

A horizontal assistant knows a little about everything and owns nothing. A vertical system knows one domain cold: its documents, its regulations, its edge cases, the decisions its people make all day. That depth is exactly what a general model cannot buy off the shelf, because it lives in proprietary data and hard-won workflow, not in the weights.

Depth also compounds. A vertical system that sees a domain's data and outcomes every day gets sharper in a way a general tool never will. The moat is not the model. It is the domain loop wrapped around it.

THE STRATEGIC READ

For a buyer: a horizontal copilot is a rent, identical to what everyone rents. A vertical capability on your proprietary data is an asset, harder to build and yours. Want advantage over parity? Fund depth.

For a builder: do not build a thinner general assistant. Pick one industry, one painful workflow, own it end to end. A narrow product a specialist cannot live without beats a broad one they can take or leave.

WHAT TO DO NOW

1. Ask what only you know. Your data and domain workflow are the raw material of a moat.

2. Prefer deep over broad. One workflow owned beats ten touched shallowly.

3. Judge AI products on domain fit, not feature lists.

Horizontal AI made intelligence a utility. Vertical AI is where you turn it into an advantage.

So: what does your industry know that a general model never will, and who is building on it, you or someone else?
