Ten AI startups I would build if I were 25 again. Ideas are cheap. The wedge behind each one is the real gift.

What is scarce is not an idea, it is a wedge: a specific reason a small team can win against incumbents and general models. These lean toward Bharat, where the problems are large and the tools are finally good enough.

1. Vernacular voice agents for the frontline. People speak, not type, in a dozen languages. A wedge global players will not prioritize.

2. Compliance back office for regulated SMEs. GST, filings, audits. Painful, rule-bound, high volume. Dull and durable.

3. Agentic leverage for solo legal and accounting practices. Give a two-person firm the reach of twenty.

4. Diagnostic vision for under-served clinics. Screening from images where specialists are scarce, built with traceability.

5. An AI layer over informal credit. Underwriting the thin-file borrower from consented alternative data. A real local data moat.

6. Agri intelligence per plot, not per region. Sowing, disease and market timing for a farmer's own field, by voice.

7. A workflow-owning agent for one boring vertical (freight, claims, logistics docs). Sell the outcome, not the model.

8. On-device AI for privacy-sensitive work. Where data cannot leave the building, small models win by default.

9. An eval and reliability platform for AI teams. When everyone builds agents, help them trust the agents.

10. Talent infrastructure for the AI era. Map real skills to real work and close the gap. This one I am building, as GuildTrek.

THE PATTERN

Each owns a workflow, not a model. Each sits on data a general model cannot reach. Each solves a painful, high-volume problem for a specific user. None is "a better chatbot." That is the wedge.

So which of these is sitting in your industry, waiting for someone who understands the domain to build it?
