For two days I have argued that AI is moving faster than we can absorb. Here is
where that ends if we are not careful. A large share of today's AI spend will never
show up as value. Some of it will quietly be written off. The only question worth
asking is whether any of it is yours.

## Hype funds the future ahead of its time

Hype is not lying. It is just early. It pulls investment forward, ahead of the value
the technology can actually realize this year. That is not always wrong, some of it
is how the future gets built. But when the gap between what you spent and what you
can show persists, it does not disappear. It gets written down: the abandoned
platform, the unused licenses, the reorganization that produced slides instead of
outcomes.

We have seen this pattern before. The dot-com era was right about the internet and
catastrophically wrong about the timing and the price. The technology was real. The
valuations were not. A lot of correct bets still lost money, because they were made
too early and too expensively.

## This is not doom, it is discipline

Let me be clear, because I build this stuff and believe in it. AI is real, and the
value is real. But value realized is not the same as value promised, and the
difference is a number on a balance sheet. The firms that come through this well are
not the ones that spent the most or the least. They are the ones that treated AI as
capital allocation with discipline, not FOMO with a budget.

That means naming the outcome in a real unit, funding the ability to absorb as hard
as the ability to adopt, and being honest about which bets are grounded and which
are faith.

## The question to sit with

So before your next AI budget, one exercise. Split your AI investments into two
lists: the ones you could defend in a downturn, with a number, and the ones you are
carrying on faith. If the second list is longer than the first, you are not
investing in AI. You are buying hype, and hype is exactly the thing that gets
written down.

The technology is not the risk. Getting drunk on it is. Think again.
