For two days I have argued AI is moving faster than we can absorb. Here is where that ends if we are not careful.

A large share of today's AI spend will never show up as value. Some of it will quietly be written off. The only question worth asking is whether any of it is yours.

HYPE FUNDS THE FUTURE AHEAD OF ITS TIME

Hype is not lying. It is early. It pulls investment forward, ahead of the value the technology can realize this year. When the gap between what you spent and what you can show persists, it does not vanish. It gets written down: the abandoned platform, the unused licenses, the reorg that produced slides instead of outcomes.

We have seen this before. The dot-com era was right about the internet and catastrophically wrong about the timing and the price. The technology was real. The valuations were not.

THIS IS NOT DOOM, IT IS DISCIPLINE

I build this stuff and believe in it. AI is real, and the value is real. But value realized is not value promised, and the difference is a number on a balance sheet. The firms that come through well treat AI as capital allocation with discipline, not FOMO with a budget.

One exercise before your next AI budget. Split your AI investments into two lists: the ones you could defend in a downturn, with a number, and the ones you are carrying on faith. If the second list is longer, you are not investing in AI. You are buying hype, and hype is what gets written down.

The technology is not the risk. Getting drunk on it is.

Think again.
